Prop Firm Portal

Firm Portal

A dedicated, separate login for prop firm organizations — distinct from the public trader-facing side — where firms manage their presence, promotions, and customer responses.

Core Functions
  • Respond to comments — reply to user reviews, both positive and negative.
  • Upload promotions — add and manage incoming promotional offers.
  • Raffle / giveaway supply — provide accounts or custom coupon codes for giveaways.
  • Coupon auto-redemption — winner enters a raffle-specific custom coupon on the firm's own site, which auto-registers the funded account with no payment required.
Confirmed Add-Ons
  • Verified Firm Badge / Claim-Your-Profile — email or domain verification before a firm can respond or post promos; blocks impersonation.
  • Promo Performance Dashboard — views, clicks, and redemptions per promotion, coupon, and raffle entry.
  • Coupon Inventory & Expiry Management — quantity caps, start/end dates, auto-deactivation of expired or fully-redeemed codes.
  • Response SLA & Notifications — alerts on new reviews (especially negative) plus a public "average response time" metric.
  • Firm Profile Content Block — firm-maintained profit split, drawdown rules, payout speed, platforms, fees, account sizes (subject to approval).
  • Raffle Fulfillment Tracking — winner-to-account/coupon log with redemption and delivery confirmation.
  • Tiered Firm Accounts — Free (respond + claim) vs. Featured/paid (priority placement, banners, pinned raffles).
  • Dispute / Flag-a-Review — firms flag suspected fake or defamatory reviews for moderation, with no delete rights.
  • API / Affiliate Link Handshake — standardized tracked referral links doubling as a revenue stream.

02

Penny-Auction Page (Token Bidding)

Auctions / Monetization

A page that auctions top prop firm accounts using a paid-token bidding model, with a resetting final-day countdown to drive engagement while the user base is still growing.

Mechanics
  • Token purchase — users buy tokens from the site (e.g., $0.10–$0.50 each); token packs in bundles (e.g., $10 → 40 tokens @ $0.25).
  • One token = one bid — each bid raises the account price by a fixed increment.
  • Promo window — auction runs multi-day (e.g., 7 days) to build momentum.
  • Resetting timer — final day triggers a 30-second countdown that restarts on every new bid.
  • Winner — when the timer hits zero with no new bid, the highest bidder wins the account.
⚠ Open Legal Flag — resolve at game-plan

Pay-to-bid auctions where losing bidders forfeit token cost may qualify as an illegal lottery or gambling in some U.S. states. Solvable via buy-it-now escape valves, token refunds, or sweepstakes framing. Logged now so it's addressed before build, not after.

Tool / Data Feed

A real-time tool comparing live trading spreads across firms — the competitor's strongest differentiator and hardest-to-copy feature.
